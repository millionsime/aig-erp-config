2. THE TWO APPROVAL PIPELINES (what the system enforces)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A) PURCHASE ORDER approval - the path depends on the ORDER TOTAL:

   Direct Purchase  (under ETB 20,000)
       Procurement Officer -> [Submit for Direct Purchase] -> APPROVED

   Proforma  (ETB 20,000 - 750,000)
       Procurement Officer -> [Submit for Committee Review]
         -> Purchase Committee -> [Approve]
         -> ENTERPRISE HEAD -> [Approve] -> APPROVED
       (The Enterprise Head is the FINAL approver on this tier, and the step
        cannot be skipped. This is the core policy fix.)

   Bulky / Open Tender  (over ETB 750,000)
       Procurement Officer -> [Submit for Committee Review]
         -> Purchase Committee -> [Approve]
         -> Corporate -> [Approve]
         -> CEO -> [Approve] -> APPROVED

   At any pending step the approver may instead click [Reject] -> Rejected.

B) PAYMENT ENTRY approval - a 4-signature chain (any amount):
       Finance -> [Submit for Deputy Review]
         -> Deputy -> [Approve]
         -> Corporate -> [Approve]
         -> CEO -> [Approve] -> APPROVED
   On submit, a "Three-Way Match" server script checks that the payment is
   backed by a Purchase Order + a goods receipt (Model 19) + a supplier
   Purchase Invoice for the same amount. If any leg is missing, submit is blocked.

restore fresh Draft documents for another run, execute on the host:

   wsl -d Ubuntu -- bash -lc "docker exec -w /home/frappe/frappe-bench \
     frappe_docker-backend-1 bench --site frontend execute \
     custom_theme.aig_runner.run --kwargs \"{'path':'/tmp/18_demodata.py'}\""

Note: script 18 is idempotent - it will not duplicate the payment chain while the
marker payment (reference_no "AIGDEMO-LIVE-400K") still exists. To fully reset,
first delete/cancel ACC-PAY-2026-00003 and the draft POs, then re-run.

Alternatively, the fastest reset for live rehearsal is to keep the already-
APPROVED examples (Section 3, bottom) for "here is a completed flow" and drive
only ONE Draft document per tier on stage.

6. TROUBLESHOOTING (seen during rehearsal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: "I logged in as a committee member but there is no action button, and I get
   'User don't have permissions to select/read this account'."
   TWO separate things, both resolved:
   (1) WRONG DOCUMENT. The committee approves PURCHASE ORDERS, not payments.
       The committee has NO role in the Payment Entry workflow, so opening the
       Payment Entry ACC-PAY-2026-00003 shows no button - that is correct.
       -> As committee, open the Purchase Order that is "Pending Purchase
          Committee Approval" (right now PUR-ORD-2026-00005). Approve/Reject
          appear top-right. Verified: committee1 gets ['Approve','Reject'] there.
   (2) ACCOUNT PERMISSION (fixed). The account error came from ERPNext's
       get_party_account(), which runs whenever a Payment Entry or Purchase
       Invoice form loads its party. The AIG approver roles were missing read
       permission on the Account doctype. This has been fixed: Account read is
       now granted to AIG Deputy, CEO, Enterprise Head, Purchase Committee and
       Evaluation Committee (Corporate/Finance/Procurement already had it).
       Verified: finance, deputy, corporate, ceo, committee1 and head.agro can
       all open the party account with no error. 195 orphaned permission rows
       (left by an earlier build bug) were also cleaned up.
   NOTE: the payment chain is approved by Finance -> Deputy -> Corporate -> CEO.
   The committee is not part of it.

Q: Payment submit crashed with '"_tol" is an invalid variable name'.
   FIXED. The live Three-Way Match Server Script had reverted to an old v2 that
   used an illegal underscore name and demanded a PO reference. It has been
   redeployed as v3 (RestrictedPython-safe, PI-centric) and re-verified: the
   demo payment ACC-PAY-2026-00003 (ETB 400,000) reconciles against invoice
   ACC-PINV-2026-00002 -> PO PUR-ORD-2026-00007 -> receipt MAT-PRE-2026-00002,
   and the PE is still in Draft, ready to run live.

Q: I already consumed the Draft docs while testing - how do I get fresh ones?

   PUR-ORD-2026-00005 Pending Purchase Committee Approval, PUR-ORD-2026-00006
