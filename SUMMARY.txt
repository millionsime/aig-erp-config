================================================================================
AIG Procurement & Cost Center Demo - ONE-PAGE SUMMARY (plain language)
================================================================================

WHAT WAS BUILT
We configured your existing ERPNext (v16) system - company "Adama Investment
Group", currency ETB - to match the AIG procurement policy, using ONLY the
safe configuration layer. Nothing inside the ERPNext or Frappe program code was
changed, so a future ERPNext upgrade will not break any of this.

THE PEOPLE AND THEIR ACCESS
- 8 new roles describe who can do what: AIG CEO, Corporate, Deputy, Enterprise
  Head, Purchase Committee, Evaluation Committee, Finance, Procurement Officer.
- 11 demo users (e.g. ceo@aig.local, head.agro@aig.local) each hold the right
  role(s). Shared demo password: aig2026.
- Each enterprise head can only see and act on their own enterprise's work.
  This is done with "User Permissions" tied to cost centers - so the Agro head
  literally cannot approve a Construction purchase.

THE MONEY MAP (COST CENTERS)
- A cost-center tree (17 nodes) splits spending into three enterprises: Agro,
  Construction, and Integrated Service, each with its own sub-lines (Dairy Farm,
  Poultry Farm, Fuel Station, etc.). Every purchase must carry a cost center.

THE APPROVAL RULES (THE IMPORTANT PART)
Two approval pipelines now run automatically:
1. Purchase Order approval - decides who must sign based on the amount:
     - Under ETB 20,000 (Direct Purchase): a fast, single path to Approved.
     - ETB 20,000 - 750,000 (Proforma): Committee -> ENTERPRISE HEAD -> Approved.
       The Enterprise Head is the FINAL, MANDATORY approver on this tier and the
       step cannot be skipped or substituted - this was the core defect we fixed.
     - Over ETB 750,000 (Bulky / Open Tender): Committee -> Corporate -> CEO ->
       Approved (the Enterprise Head is NOT on this tier - it escalates to the
       corporate signatories instead).
2. Payment approval - a 4-signature chain: Deputy -> Corporate -> CEO ->
   Approved. A payment is also blocked until the "three-way match" passes: the
   Purchase Order, the goods receipt (Model 19), and the supplier invoice must
   all line up with the amount being paid.

GUARDRAILS
- Budgets are live and enforced: Dairy Farm (ETB 2M) and Fuel Station (500K)
  warn when exceeded; Poultry Farm (50K) STOPS an over-budget purchase.
- Two "guard" scripts prevent anyone from submitting a document behind the
  workflow's back (a raw submit is rejected unless it went through approvals).
- Supplier bank details are locked down: only CEO, Corporate, Deputy and Finance
  can read that field (Section 9 requirement).

TAX
- Two withholding categories (7.5% VAT withholding, 3% withholding tax) and two
  purchase tax templates (AIG VAT 15%, Ethiopia Tax) are set up.

READY FOR THE DEMO
A complete, realistic story is already on the system: three approved purchase
orders (Direct 15K, Proforma 300K, Bulky 1M), a goods receipt, a supplier
invoice, and an approved payment - so you can click through a real end-to-end
flow live on Saturday.

PROOF IT WORKS
An automated acceptance suite runs the whole story and checks the rules:
24 out of 24 checks PASS, including "the Enterprise Head gate cannot be skipped",
"another enterprise's head cannot approve", "over-budget purchases are stopped",
and "supplier banking stays restricted".

SAFETY PROMISE
All work is additive and reversible through the config layer. No existing
DocType, field, role, or user was deleted or renamed, and no core frappe/erpnext
file was modified.
