0. WHY THIS LOOKS DIFFERENT FROM THE LEGACY FILE
The data-quality review found ~276 accounts with duplicates (Salaries x4,
Depreciation-Building copies, 5 identical "Paid up capital" lines, party
names as GL accounts, two Retained Earnings codes, malformed codes). ERPNext
needs ONE natural account per economic thing; the enterprise dimension lives
in the COST CENTER on each transaction (already enforced), and per-party
detail lives in Customer/Supplier/Employee masters. The table below is the
cleaned target; legacy-code mapping happens at migration, not by re-creating
legacy duplicates.

4. LEGACY MAPPING PRINCIPLES (for the migration spreadsheet)
- N duplicate natural accounts (Salaries x4 etc.) -> the ONE live account;
  entity distinction = Cost Center on each historical transaction.
- Party-named GL accounts -> Customer/Supplier/Employee master + the
  control account; opening per-party balances go into the masters
  (ERPNext imports party-wise opening balances natively).
- "Paid up capital" x5 -> per-shareholder accounts once labelled (#3).
- 30003 / 3200 Retained Earnings -> single RE; authoritative-balance answer
  needed (#4) before migration.
- Malformed codes (4-digit, stray decimal, missing digit) -> the 5-digit
  scheme in 3.7; codes are cosmetic in ERPNext (names are the keys).
- Every legacy account must map to exactly one live account or one PROPOSED
  account in section 3; anything unmapped goes to "Temporary Opening" for
  AIG Finance to triage - never silently dropped.
