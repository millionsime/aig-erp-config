PART A - THE SIX-ITEM CONFIRMATION LIST (brief Section 11)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These six points were ambiguous in the source policy. Below is the decision that
was IMPLEMENTED on the instance, so it can be reviewed and, if needed, adjusted
before Saturday. Each is a config-layer change (Workflow / Role / Server Script)
and can be re-pointed without touching core code.

1) "Enterprise Head" vs "Enterprise CEO" - same role or different?
   DECISION IMPLEMENTED: treated as ONE role, "AIG Enterprise Head", representing
   the head of each enterprise (Agro, Construction, Integrated Service). The
   separate corporate-level signatories are the distinct roles AIG Corporate and
   AIG CEO. No separate "Enterprise CEO" role was created.
   TO CONFIRM: is a distinct Enterprise CEO signature required in addition to the
   Enterprise Head? (Currently: no.)

2) Does the Enterprise Head approve on the BULKY / Open Tender tier (> 750,000)?
   DECISION IMPLEMENTED: NO. As built, the Enterprise Head is the FINAL approver
   on the Proforma tier only (Committee -> Enterprise Head -> Approved). On the
   Bulky tier the chain escalates past the Enterprise Head to the corporate
   signatories: Committee -> Corporate -> CEO -> Approved. The Enterprise Head
   step is bypassed only on the Direct Purchase tier (< 20,000) and on Bulky.
   TO CONFIRM: is that the intended reading? If AIG wants the Enterprise Head to
   ALSO sign on the Bulky tier (e.g. Committee -> Enterprise Head -> Corporate ->
   CEO), that is a one-line Workflow Transition change - flag it and I will add it.

3) Committee approval - UNANIMOUS or ANY-ONE member?
   DECISION IMPLEMENTED: ANY-ONE. A single user holding the AIG Purchase
   Committee role can move the document from "Pending Purchase Committee
   Approval" to the next state. The workflow engine records one transition.
   (The three committee demo users exist to show the membership, but the current
   model does not require all three to sign.)
   TO CONFIRM: if unanimity (all members must sign) is required, this needs an
   extra mechanism (e.g. a child-table sign-off + Server Script) - flag now.

4) "Model 42" representation.
   DECISION IMPLEMENTED: the goods-receipt document that evidences physical
   receipt (the "Model 19" receipt in the policy) is represented by the standard
   ERPNext Purchase Receipt (demo: MAT-PRE-2026-00001). The three-way match keys
   off this submitted Purchase Receipt. No separate custom "Model 42" DocType was
   created, to stay additive and upgrade-safe.
   TO CONFIRM: is a distinct Model 42 form (separate DocType / print format)
   required for the demo, or is the Purchase Receipt sufficient as the receipt
   evidence?

5) Exact tax withholding rules.
   DECISION IMPLEMENTED: two Tax Withholding Categories were created as named in
   the policy - "AIG 7.5% VAT Withholding" and "AIG 3% Withholding Tax" - plus
   two Purchase Taxes and Charges Templates ("AIG VAT 15% (Purchase) - AIG",
   "Ethiopia Tax - AIG"). The precise thresholds, cumulative limits, and the
   accounts each posts to were set to sensible demo defaults.
   TO CONFIRM: the exact statutory rates, per-supplier thresholds, and GL
   accounts for withholding, so the demo figures match AIG's real tax rules.

6) Purchase Committee - PER-ENTERPRISE or CROSS-ENTERPRISE?
   DECISION IMPLEMENTED: CROSS-ENTERPRISE (one committee role that can review any
   enterprise's Proforma/Bulky purchase). Enterprise separation is enforced one
   step later, at the Enterprise Head gate, via cost-center User Permissions.
   TO CONFIRM: should the committee itself be split per enterprise (an Agro
   committee, a Construction committee, etc.)?

PART B - KEY DESIGN DECISIONS / DEVIATIONS (flagged for transparency)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1) Pending workflow states carry docstatus = 1 (submitted).
    Reason: Frappe v16 forbids a Draft(docstatus 0) -> Rejected(docstatus 2)
    transition, and allow_edit is mandatory per state. The Purchase Order is
    therefore submitted when it enters committee review. Rejection moves it to
    docstatus 2. This is engine behaviour, not a policy change.

D2) Enterprise cost center is a CUSTOM mandatory field (Purchase Order.
    aig_cost_center, Link -> Cost Center, reqd 1) rather than making the native
    cost_center field mandatory.
    Reason: avoids editing core field properties; fully upgrade-safe and
    additive.

D3) Supplier banking restriction is done with a permlevel-1 CUSTOM FIELD
    (Supplier.aig_bank_details) + permlevel-1 Custom DocPerm, NOT with a Property
    Setter on native Supplier fields.
    Reason: keeps the restriction in the config layer and reversible.

D4) FOUR Server Scripts are in use (all flagged in CHANGELOG section 6):
    three-way match, PO cost-center default, PO raw-submit guard, PE raw-submit
    guard. Reason: these behaviours cannot be expressed by Workflow alone and
    must not be added to core code.

D5) Budgets are SUBMITTED (docstatus = 1). Reason: ERPNext only enforces a budget
    once it is submitted; unsubmitted budgets are ignored by the budget check.
