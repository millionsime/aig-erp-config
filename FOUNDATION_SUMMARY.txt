================================================================================
AIG ERPNext FOUNDATION - ONE-PAGE SUMMARY (plain language)
================================================================================

WHAT WAS BUILT
The accounting foundation underneath your ERPNext (v16) system - company,
fiscal years, the chart of accounts corrections, the enterprise cost-center
tree, and the rules that make every balance-sheet number traceable to the
enterprise that owns it. All of it sits in the safe configuration layer:
nothing inside the ERPNext or Frappe program code was touched, so a future
upgrade will not break any of this.

THE COMPANY AND ITS YEARS
One company: Adama Investment Group (AIG), Ethiopian Birr, Ethiopia. Two
fiscal years now exist under Ethiopian-labels: "FY 2018 EC (Jul 2025 - Jul
2026)" and "FY 2019 EC (Jul 2026 - Jul 2027)". The Ethiopian year is in the
name so staff recognize it; the dates inside are the matching Gregorian
ranges (8 July to 7 July), which is what the system actually uses.
NOTE: this assumes the standard Ethiopian-government convention (Hamle 1 to
Sene 30). AIG Finance must confirm AIG truly starts its year on 1 Hamle -
some organizations start on Meskerem 1 instead. One confirmation changes
the dates; nothing else breaks.

THE CHART OF ACCOUNTS
Good news: the duplicated 276-account file from the data-quality review was
NEVER imported. The live system runs ERPNext's standard chart (99 accounts),
which already has what the review asked for: one Salaries, one Depreciation,
one Bank Charges, customer/vendor control accounts, and exactly one Retained
Earnings. We fixed one real defect: your bank account (Commercial Bank of
Ethiopia) had been sitting under LIABILITIES - the wrong side of the balance
sheet. It now sits under a new "Bank Accounts" group in Assets, with its
balance intact. A consolidated chart draft is attached for AIG Finance to
sign off before real opening balances are migrated from the actual books.

THE ENTERPRISE MAP
The cost-center tree now matches the structure decision exactly, with one
consistent naming style ([Enterprise] - AIG): Agro Enterprise (Dairy,
Poultry, Slaughterhouse, Animal Feed Factory), Construction Enterprise
(Construction Projects), Integrated Service Enterprise (Fuel Station, City
Mall, Garage, Parking, Cafeteria and Parks), plus Head Office. The stray
"Main", "Tech" and "Engineering" nodes are switched off (not deleted).

EVERY BALANCE SHEET LINE KNOWS ITS ENTERPRISE (THE BIG ONE)
Cost Center is now effectively mandatory on purchase invoices, sales
invoices, payments, and journal-entry lines - with automatic defaults from
the company so normal work stays fast. This is what makes the per-enterprise
Balance Sheet work, and it was tested, not assumed:
  - Depreciation test: a machine tagged to Dairy Farm depreciated itself,
    and the Accumulated Depreciation entry (a balance-sheet account) carried
    Dairy Farm's tag automatically. Confirmed in the ledger itself.
  - Balance Sheet filtered to Dairy Farm shows exactly the Dairy figures;
    cleared filter shows the correct consolidated total. Both verified
    against the raw ledger.
  - Budget Variance: a test expense posted to Dairy appeared as Dairy
    consumption immediately, and disappeared cleanly when cancelled.

THE ETHIOPIAN CALENDAR - HONEST SCOPE
Purchase orders, journal entries, payments, and attendance now SHOW the
Ethiopian (Ge'ez) date next to the working date - e.g. "25 Nehase 2018 EC".
This is a reference display only. The date the system posts on, the fiscal
year it lands in, and every approval deadline remain Gregorian. We did not
modify the program code (the only way to make the date picker itself
Ethiopian), because that would break on every upgrade. If AIG needs the
system to natively RUN in Ethiopian dates, that is a separate custom
development decision for leadership - flagged, not attempted.

PROOF IT WORKS
A 12-point acceptance suite ran against the live system: 12 of 12 checks
PASS, including negative tests (a journal entry line without a cost center
is blocked) and clean-up tests (cancelled test entries leave no trace in
reports).

SAFETY PROMISE
No core frappe/erpnext file was modified (verified by scanning both app
trees for recently changed code files: zero). No existing record was
deleted; strays were disabled, not removed. Every rename used the standard
rename mechanism so history, budgets, permissions and references followed
automatically.
